# Hichem Benali

I started with databases and production systems. I kept following the problems.

I learn by following a problem until I can build around it. That has taken me through software, products, algorithms, research and into questions about intelligence, knowledge and how complex systems hold together.

I do not like choosing an explanation too early. I would rather keep several alive and let evidence eliminate them.

I like ambitious systems. I just want them to survive contact with reality.

Currently building **SYSTASYS**.

SYSTASYS brings research, decisions, operations and capital into one working system. The core stays private. I publish selected work when it is useful on its own.
